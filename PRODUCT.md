## Platform

web

## Users
Anyone evaluating Lucas Grecco's work: the owner described it as "a general portfolio of my works", with no single priority audience confirmed (recruiters and freelance clients were offered and not chosen). Visitors arrive from links, scan quickly, and want to see what he has built, with what, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio that presents Lucas Grecco's works (software, IoT, design), his skills and studies, and where to find him. Success: a visitor can find and open any project and its code, understand the stack behind it, and reach his profiles within seconds.

## Positioning
Full stack developer at Asaas (Skywalkers team, product invoices) and Computer Engineering student at FIAP, building web products end to end, from back-end to interface (Java, TypeScript, Python), with a background in design and agile methods. Confirmed from the owner's LinkedIn profile (2026-10-14).

## Operating Context
- Projects come from GitHub at build time: repos with the `portfolio` topic plus a short list of third-party repos; description from About (EN) and a marked README block (PT), cover image from the README, demo from the website field, with a committed snapshot as fallback.
- One page with sections: hero, about, projects, stack, path (timeline), contact; plus a 404.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages, rebuilt on push, daily and on demand.
- The visitor mostly sees the site on desktop and phone; first load must stay light (the 3D scene is a lazy chunk).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Stack: Astro with TypeScript; React only for the 3D logo island (three.js with R3F).
- Languages: Portuguese and English, with a language switch (confirmed).
- Audience: general, no priority (confirmed again 2026-10-14).
- The 3D avatar is retired; the hero centerpiece is the logo in 3D (chosen from implemented prototypes).

## Product Principles
1. The work is the content: every project is reachable in one step with its code and stack.
2. The owner is a person, not a template: the name, the symbol and the avatar carry identity.
3. Fast and legible on a phone before anything is decorative.
4. Bilingual by design: Portuguese and English are equal, not a translation afterthought.
5. Content comes from real data (GitHub) and nothing is invented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No product-specific requirement stated. Baseline kept from current work: keyboard navigation with visible focus, a skip link, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
